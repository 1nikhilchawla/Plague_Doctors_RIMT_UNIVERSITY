GlucoTwin fuses a Type 2 patient's health record with their CGM and wearable streams and shows the doctor the next two hours: on 45 real adults (CGMacros) it now beats persistence at every horizon, with 18.3% lower 2-hour error. Spike warnings arrive a median 105 minutes early in our stress test, and its low warnings are now scored blind: 6 of 16 simulated lows warned, every one of those alerts correct. Built for the Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026; research prototype, not medical advice.
