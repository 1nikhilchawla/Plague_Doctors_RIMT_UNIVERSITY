# Alert proof: warning time against simple alarms

Research prototype — not a medical device, not medical advice. Generated by `scripts/alert-proof.ts`; do not hand-edit.

Data: the simulated stress test (mismatched generator, seed 42), 11 patients × 12 scored days = 132 patient-days. scoreAlertEvents: a hit is an alert in the 120 min before a spike (60 min before a low); a false alert has no event in the following window. All methods share hygieneFires (hysteresis, 60-min suppression, 8 alerts/day cap, gaps block).

Rebuilt twin alerts match the stored evaluation: **yes** (299 spike alerts, 155 hits of 254 spikes).
