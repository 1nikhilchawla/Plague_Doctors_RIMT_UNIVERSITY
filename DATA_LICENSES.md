## CGMacros

- Authors: Anurag Das, David Kerr, Namino Glanz, Wendy Bevier, Rony Santiago, Ricardo Gutierrez-Osuna, Bobak Mortazavi
- DOI: [10.13026/3z8q-x658](https://doi.org/10.13026/3z8q-x658)
- Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- What is in the repo: `src/lib/glucotwin/real/real_eval.json` and `demo_patients.json` are computed derivatives (5-minute grid, meal macros scaled by amount consumed, walk bouts from METs). They are not a copy of the source CSVs.
- Raw files stay outside the repository. `ml/download_cgmacros.sh` fetches them locally.

## IFCT 2017

Food macros on the what-if plate are scaled from the Indian Food Composition Tables (Longvah et al., NIN–ICMR, 2017) for the verified dishes only. Unverified dishes stay disabled. GI is not in IFCT and is left blank.

## Simulated cohort

The ward is synthetic, seed 42. It is not a clinical dataset. The patient count is the `patients` field in `reports/sim_eval.json` after `npm run readme:results`. One sample Apple Health export and one FHIR bundle are under `samples/`. Those files are generated by this repository's TypeScript, not by Synthea.

## Synthea

Synthea was not run. The FHIR bundle's generator name is `glucotwin-ts-fhir`. Do not treat the sample as an ABDM or NRCeS artefact.
